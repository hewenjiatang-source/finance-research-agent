# Apple Inc. (AAPL) FY2024 Form 10-K: Key Financials and Year-over-Year Changes

## Executive summary
- **Revenue:** Total net sales were $391,035 million in FY2024 (52 weeks ended Sep 28, 2024), against $383,285 million in FY2023 [5][11]. The 10-K MD&A shows the change as 2% [9].
- **Net income:** $93,736 million in FY2024, against $96,995 million in FY2023 [5][11]. This is a computed decline of 3.36% [8].
- **Operating cash flow:** $118,254 million in FY2024, against $110,543 million in FY2023 [4].
- **Total assets:** $364,980 million at Sep 28, 2024, against $352,583 million at Sep 30, 2023 [4][11].
- **R&D expense:** $31,370 million in FY2024, against $29,915 million in FY2023, up 5% per the MD&A [9][8].
- **Why net income fell:** The MD&A attributes the higher FY2024 effective tax rate (24.1% vs 14.7%) primarily to a one-time income tax charge of $10.2 billion, net, related to the State Aid Decision [9].

## Scope
- **Company and filing:** Apple Inc. (CIK 320193), Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended September 28, 2024. It was filed 2024-11-01 under accession 0000320193-24-000123 [1].
- **Basis:** GAAP, consolidated, in USD millions.
- **Periods:**
  - FY2024 covers Oct 1, 2023 – Sep 28, 2024, a 52-week year [4][9].
  - FY2023 ended Sep 30, 2023 and was a 53-week year, with the extra week in Q1 FY2023 [9].
  - Balance sheet figures are as of Sep 28, 2024 and Sep 30, 2023 [2][4].
- **Prior-year source:** FY2023 figures are the comparatives presented in the FY2024 10-K, not taken from the FY2023 10-K [11].
- **Statement locations in the 10-K:** The Statements of Operations, Balance Sheets and Cash Flows are on printed pages 29, 31 and 33 respectively [2].

## Drivers and segment detail

**Net sales by category, FY2024 vs FY2023 ($ million) [9]:**

| Category | FY2024 | FY2023 | Change | Stated driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone | 201,183 | 200,583 | —% | Relatively flat |
| Mac | 29,984 | 29,357 | +2% | Higher laptop sales |
| iPad | 26,694 | 28,300 | −6% | Lower iPad Pro and entry-level iPad sales, partly offset by higher iPad Air sales |
| Wearables, Home and Accessories | 37,005 | 39,845 | −7% | Lower Wearables and Accessories sales |
| Services | 96,169 | 85,200 | +13% | Higher advertising, App Store and cloud services sales |

Computed Services growth was 12.87% [9].

**Net sales by segment, FY2024 vs FY2023 ($ million) [9]:**
- Americas: $167,045 vs $162,560, up 3%, primarily on higher Services sales.
- Europe: $101,328 vs $94,294, up 7%, primarily on higher Services and iPhone sales.
- Greater China: $66,952 vs $72,559, down 8%, primarily on lower iPhone and iPad sales. The weaker renminbi was unfavorable.
- Japan: $25,052 vs $24,257, up 3%, primarily on higher iPhone sales. The weaker yen was unfavorable.
- Rest of Asia Pacific: $30,658 vs $29,615, up 4%, primarily on higher Services sales. Weaker foreign currencies were unfavorable.

**Margins [9]:**
- Products gross margin was 37.2% vs 36.5%, due to cost savings, partly offset by a different Products mix and weaker foreign currencies.
- Services gross margin was 73.9% vs 70.8%, due to a different Services mix.

**Operating expenses [9]:**
- R&D growth was driven primarily by increases in headcount-related expenses.
- SG&A was $26,097 million vs $24,932 million, up 5%.
- Total operating expenses were $57,467 million vs $54,847 million.

**Tax [9]:**
- The provision for income taxes was $29,749 million vs $16,741 million, an increase of $13,008 million.
- The effective tax rate was 24.1% vs 14.7%.
- The MD&A also names a higher effective tax rate on foreign earnings and lower tax benefits from share-based compensation.

## Risks and uncertainties
- **State Aid Decision:** A tax payable of €14.2 billion ($15.8 billion) was owed to Ireland as of Sep 28, 2024. It was expected to be paid within 12 months. The funds were held in escrow and restricted from general use [9].
- **Foreign exchange:** Weaker renminbi, yen and other currencies were unfavorable to net sales in Greater China, Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific [9]. The 10-K also notes the company may choose not to hedge certain foreign currency exposures [2].
- **Greater China:** Net sales declined 8%, driven by lower iPhone and iPad sales [9].
- **Comparability:** FY2023 had 53 weeks and FY2024 had 52, so year-over-year comparisons are affected by the extra week [9].

## Data notes and limitations
- **Source of headline figures:** They come from XBRL company facts tagged to the FY2024 10-K [4][5][11]. The statement text was not read line by line for these items. Net sales, operating income and the MD&A figures were cross-checked against the 10-K text [2][9].
- **No independent second source:** None was retrieved. The filings lookup returned only 2026 8-Ks, no Q4 FY2024 earnings release [12]. No discrepancy was found, but none was tested.
- **Derived figures:**
  - The net sales, net income and R&D percentage changes were computed with a code sandbox that returned no evidence id [8][9]. They are consistent with the MD&A's 2% and 5%.
  - Percentage and dollar changes for operating cash flow and total assets were not computed, because the calculator tool failed. Only the direction is given.
  - Operating cash flow was $118,254 million vs $110,543 million, and total assets were $364,980 million vs $352,583 million [4].
- **Not quantified in the retrieved text:**
  - The 53rd-week effect on net sales.
  - The dollar size of the headcount-related R&D increase.
  - A net income figure excluding the State Aid charge.
- **Restatement check:** The FY2023 10-K, as originally filed, was not retrieved for a restatement check. Its statement locations are indexed [3], and it shows the same FY2023 net sales ($383,285 million) and operating income ($114,301 million) as the FY2024 10-K comparatives [3][2].
