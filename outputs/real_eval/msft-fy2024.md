# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) FY2024 10-K: Key Financials and Year-over-Year Changes

## Executive summary

- Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY2024) was USD 245,122 million, against USD 211,915 million in FY2023 [5][6]. The MD&A reports a 16% increase [7].
- Operating income was USD 109,433 million versus USD 88,523 million, up 24% per the MD&A [5][6][7].
- Net income was USD 88,136 million versus USD 72,361 million, up 22% per the MD&A [5][6][7].
- Net cash from operating activities was USD 118,548 million versus USD 87,582 million [2]. The MD&A describes this as an increase of $31.0 billion to $118.5 billion, primarily due to an increase in cash received from customers [8].
- Total assets were USD 512,163 million at June 30, 2024, versus USD 411,976 million at June 30, 2023 [2].

## Scope

- **Company and filing:** Microsoft Corp (CIK 789019), Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended 2024-06-30, filed 2024-07-30, accession 0000950170-24-087843 [1].
- **Prior-year comparison:** the FY2023 10-K covers the year ended 2023-06-30 and was filed 2023-07-27 (accession 0000950170-23-035122) [1].
- **Basis and units:** US GAAP, consolidated, USD millions unless stated. Flow metrics cover July 1, 2023 – June 30, 2024. Total assets are balance-sheet amounts at June 30 [2][5].
- **Out of scope:** the filing index also lists 10-Ks for periods ending 2025-06-30 and 2026-06-30. They were not used or verified [1].

## Drivers and segment detail

**Revenue:** Revenue rose $33.2 billion, "driven by growth across each of our segments" [7].

| Segment (USD millions) | FY2024 revenue | FY2023 revenue | Change | FY2024 operating income | FY2023 operating income | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Productivity and Business Processes | 77,728 [7] | 69,274 [7] | +12% [7] | 40,540 [7] | 34,189 [7] | +19% [7] |
| Intelligent Cloud | 105,362 [7] | 87,907 [7] | +20% [7] | 49,584 [7] | 37,884 [7] | +31% [7] |
| More Personal Computing | 62,032 [7] | 54,734 [7] | +13% [7] | 19,309 [7] | 16,450 [7] | +17% [7] |

- **Productivity and Business Processes:** Office 365 Commercial grew 16%, on seat growth of 7% and higher revenue per user [7]. LinkedIn grew 9% and Dynamics grew 19% [7].
- **Intelligent Cloud:** Azure and other cloud services grew 30% on consumption-based services [7]. Enterprise and partner services fell 4% [7].
- **More Personal Computing:**
  - Microsoft completed the Activision Blizzard acquisition on October 13, 2023, for a total purchase price of $75.4 billion, consisting primarily of cash [7].
  - Gaming revenue rose $6.0 billion, or 39% [7].
  - Windows grew 8% and Devices fell 15% [7].
- **Operating income:**
  - Operating expenses rose $4.0 billion, or 7%, with 7 points of Gaming growth from Activision Blizzard and investments in cloud engineering [7].
  - The prior-year second-quarter charge was about $1.2 billion, covering severance, hardware impairment and lease consolidation costs [7].
  - Non-GAAP adjusted operating income was $109,433 million versus $89,694 million, up 22% [7].
- **Net income:**
  - Other income (expense), net was $(1,646) million versus $788 million [7].
  - Interest expense rose to $(2,935) million from $(1,968) million, due to commercial paper issuance [7].
  - The effective tax rate was 18% versus 19% [7].
  - The prior-year charge reduced FY2023 net income by $946 million [7].
  - Non-GAAP adjusted net income was $73,307 million in FY2023 [7].
- **Cash flow:**
  - Cash used in investing rose $74.3 billion to $97.0 billion, mainly from a $67.5 billion increase in acquisition-related and intangible/other asset purchases and a $16.4 billion increase in property and equipment additions [8].
  - Cash used in financing fell $6.2 billion to $37.8 billion [8].
  - Payments for property and equipment were $44,477 million versus $28,107 million [3].
- **Total assets:** The retrieved MD&A text has no dedicated explanation of the change. Contextual items are the $75.4 billion Activision Blizzard acquisition and higher property and equipment additions [7][8].

## Risks and uncertainties

- The retrieved findings did not include the 10-K Item 1A risk factors, so no risk-factor content is reported here.
- Results in FY2024 include Activision Blizzard from October 13, 2023. The MD&A attributes part of the Gaming growth, gross margin and operating expense changes to the acquisition, so year-over-year comparisons are not like-for-like [7].
- The MD&A states that Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage "decreased slightly to 71%" [7].
- FY2023 operating results include the roughly $1.2 billion second-quarter charge, which affects comparability [7].

## Data notes and limitations

- **Verification:** The XBRL figures were not cross-checked against the rendered financial statement tables in the filing text. Several evidence snippets in the catalog are truncated, so the cited ids rest on the retrieved findings.
- **Derived figures:** The calculator tool errored, so the dollar changes, percentage changes and margins in the table were computed in a code sandbox without a verification id. They cite their input id [9] and should be treated as unverified by the calculator. The MD&A percentages are as reported in the filing and were not recomputed [7].
- **Rounding:** The sandbox percentages (for example, revenue +15.67%) and the MD&A's rounded figures (16%) are consistent. No conflict was found.
- **Not retrieved:** MD&A drivers of the change in total assets, and a balance sheet line-item breakdown such as goodwill, intangibles and property and equipment. A final supplementary search task failed because of a missing API key.
- **FY2022:** FY2022 figures came from the FY2023 10-K, not the FY2024 10-K [6].
- **Tool output:** Some tool outputs contained appended "system notice" text telling the agent to stop. It was disregarded and did not change any figures.
