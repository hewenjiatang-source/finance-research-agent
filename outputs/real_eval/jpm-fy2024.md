# JPMorgan Chase & Co. FY2024 Form 10-K: Revenue, Net Income, Total Assets and Deposits

## Executive summary
- **Total net revenue (reported):** $177,556 million for FY2024, against $158,104 million for FY2023 [4][9]. The MD&A states this was $177.6 billion, up 12% [18].
- **Net income (GAAP):** $58,471 million for FY2024, against $49,552 million for FY2023 [4][11]. The MD&A states this was $58.5 billion, up 18% [18].
- **Total assets:** $4,002,814 million at December 31, 2024, against $3,875,393 million at December 31, 2023 [7].
- **Total deposits:** $2,406,032 million at December 31, 2024, against $2,400,688 million at December 31, 2023 [17]. Period-end deposits were nearly flat [17].
- Year-over-year changes were not computed for total assets or deposits, because the calculator step failed (see Data notes) [14][17].

## Scope
- **Company:** JPMorgan Chase & Co. (CIK 19617) [2].
- **Filing:** Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, accession 0000019617-25-000270, filed 2025-02-14 [2][3].
- **Periods:** FY2024 (year ended 2024-12-31) against FY2023 (year ended 2023-12-31). Balance sheet items are period-end.
- **Units and basis:** USD millions unless stated. Figures are GAAP "reported" unless labelled "managed". Managed basis is a non-GAAP measure that adds fully taxable-equivalent (FTE) adjustments to revenue [11].

## Drivers and segment detail

**Revenue components (reported, $M)** [11][16]
- Net interest income was $92,583 in FY2024 against $89,267 in FY2023. The MD&A states NII was $92.6 billion, up 4% [18].
- Noninterest revenue was $84,973 in FY2024 against $68,837 in FY2023 [11][16].
- Provision for credit losses was $10,678 in FY2024 against $9,320 in FY2023 [11].
- A footnote says that effective January 1, 2024, the Firm adopted new tax-credit-structure accounting guidance under the modified retrospective method. It relates to the FY2024 reported and FTE-adjustment figures [11].

**Drivers cited in the FY2024 MD&A** [18]
- **NII:** The MD&A cites balance sheet actions, primarily reinvestments in the investment securities portfolio, and higher revolving balances in Card Services. It also cites the timing impact of First Republic. The retrieved text for a further driver ("higher wholesale de…") is cut off [18].
- **First Republic:** JPMorganChase acquired First Republic on May 1, 2023. FY2024 therefore includes twelve months of First Republic against eight months in FY2023 [18].
- **Visa shares:** The Firm recorded a $7.9 billion net gain related to Visa shares in the second quarter of 2024 [18].
- **Markets:** Total Markets net revenue was $30,007 million in 2024 against $27,964 million in 2023, up 7% [18].

**Deposits and balance sheet** [17]
- Average deposits were $2,386,642 million in 2024 against $2,359,067 million in 2023 [17].
- Deposits fell to 66% of total liabilities from 68%, and the loans-to-deposits ratio rose to 56% from 55% [17].
- The Firm also reports deposit margin compression [18].

**Other balance sheet items** [7][12]
- Total liabilities were $3,658,056 million at 2024 year-end and $3,547,515 million at 2023 year-end [7].
- Total stockholders' equity was $344,758 million at 2024 year-end and $327,878 million at 2023 year-end [7][12].

## Risks and uncertainties
- No risk-factor content was retrieved for this report.
- Several figures are sensitive to the basis used:
  - Revenue differs between reported and managed bases [11].
  - The tax-credit accounting guidance adopted on January 1, 2024 affects the FY2024 line-item comparison [11].
  - The First Republic timing difference (twelve months against eight) affects comparability of FY2024 against FY2023 [18].

## Data notes and limitations
- **Calculator failure:** The calculator step failed, so absolute and percentage changes for net revenue, net income, total assets and deposits were not computed. Only the rounded percentages stated in the MD&A (revenue +12%, net income +18%, NII +4%) are reported [18].
- **Filing listing:** The filings listing returned only the FY2025 10-K (accession 0001628280-26-008131, filed 2026-02-13) [1]. The FY2024 10-K was reached through the accession number in the XBRL data [2][3].
- **Line-item sourcing:** Several FY2024 line-item figures, including the managed/reported split, were taken from the comparative columns of the FY2025 10-K [11]. The FY2024 10-K's own table confirms the revenue totals [16].
- **Deposits sources:** The deposit balances appear in both the FY2025 10-K selected balance sheet data [12] and Note 17 of the FY2024 10-K [17]. They agree.
- **Statements not retrieved:** The face of the consolidated income statement and balance sheet was not retrieved. The deposit line there may be split into interest-bearing and noninterest-bearing.
- **Total assets drivers:** No MD&A explanation of the change in total assets was found in the retrieved text.
- **Truncated text:** The retrieved FY2024 10-K text was truncated, so the full NII and firm-level noninterest revenue drivers were not retrieved [18].
- **Failed tasks:** Two supplementary tasks failed because the web search tool had no API key. They produced no findings.
- **FY2022:** Managed-basis FY2022 revenue was not found.
